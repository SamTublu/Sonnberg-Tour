The story

A district in motion

Sonnenberg’s story is closely connected to the growth of Chemnitz. From the mid-19th century, factories developed along the Dresdner and Augustusburger roads while workers’ housing spread across the hillside. By around 1900 it had become a densely built working-class district.

The neighbourhood changed again through the 20th century. Some historic buildings were lost or replaced, while many Gründerzeit streets were later renovated. Today, older architecture and newer cultural activity exist side by side.
